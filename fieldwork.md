# Fieldwork Notes — Local Mess Visit

**Date of Visit:** November 17, 2026  
**Duration:** ~22 minutes  
**Location:** Shri Sai Tiffin Centre, Near Patel Chowk  
**Vendor:** Ramesh Bhaidas Patel (Ramesh Bhai)

## Background & Setup
- Operates a 40-seat mess open for lunch and dinner.
- Sells fixed thalis. No digital billing or POS system; all records are kept manually or mentally.
- Main operational concern is balancing food waste against running out of meals during peak hours.

## Key Observations & Quotes
1. **Monday Demand:** Ramesh noted that Mondays consistently have high footfall because customers prefer home-cooked style meals after the weekend. He usually preps 10-12 extra thalis on Mondays.
2. **Mid-Week Uncertainty:** Wednesday and Thursday demand fluctuates unpredictably between 50 and 70 customers, causing anxiety over preparation quantities.
3. **Weekend Drop-off:** Saturday afternoons see a sharp drop in footfall after 1 PM, leading to surplus food that is often given away to local workers.

## Insights
- **Rainy Day / Delivery App Paradox:** Ramesh observed that while he traditionally over-prepares on rainy days assuming people stay indoors, recent months show mixed patterns. On some evenings, informal delivery aggregators from nearby apartments pick up slack, creating unexpected demand spikes that standard gut-feel forecasting misses.
